Solar Street Lights

Lighting for roads, pathways, and open areas.

Brighten Larger Outdoor Spaces

Solar street lights use a solar panel to collect sunlight during the day. The energy is stored in a rechargeable battery and used to power the LED light after sunset.

They can be considered for locations where running electrical cables may be difficult or expensive.

Common locations
  • Private roads and subdivisions
  • Driveways and parking areas
  • Community pathways
  • Open outdoor spaces

Things to Consider

Sunlight Exposure

Choose a location where the panel can receive enough direct sunlight.

Lighting Coverage

Consider the size of the area that needs illumination.

Installation

Follow the manufacturer's instructions for mounting and setup.
