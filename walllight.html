Solar Wall Lights

Extra light for entrances and exterior walls.

Light Up the Outside of Your Home

Solar wall lights are mounted on exterior walls. Their solar panels collect sunlight during the day, storing energy in a battery for nighttime use.

Some models include motion sensors or different lighting modes. Features depend on the specific product.

Common locations
  • Front doors and entrances
  • Garden walls and fences
  • Garages and gates
  • Patios and balconies

Benefits of Wall-Mounted Solar Lights

They can help brighten dark corners and entryways without requiring a nearby household power outlet. Before installing, check that the panel will receive enough sunlight and that the wall is suitable for mounting.
