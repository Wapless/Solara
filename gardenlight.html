Solar Garden Lights

A warm glow for gardens, paths, and outdoor spaces.

Make Your Garden More Welcoming

Solar garden lights are designed to illuminate outdoor decorations, plants, and walkways. They collect sunlight during the day and use stored energy to provide light after sunset.

They are available in different shapes and styles, from small pathway stakes to decorative lanterns.

Common locations
  • Garden paths and walkways
  • Flower beds and lawns
  • Outdoor seating areas
  • Patios and landscaping

Simple Placement Tips

Place garden lights where they can receive sunlight and where their light will be useful. Avoid positioning the solar panel under thick shade or where it will be covered by plants.
